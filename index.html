    From PDF to plain text

    1. Choose your PDFs

      Drop in up to 50 files, or use “Choose PDF files”. Each file can be up to 100 MB, with 300 MB total per batch.

    2. Extract and preview

      Click “Extract text”, then choose a file to see its text. Adjust line breaks or page numbers at any time.

    3. Take your text with you

      Copy it, download one .txt file, or download all results together in a ZIP. All text files use UTF-8.

    Your files are processed in this browser and aren’t saved by the app. Download your results before closing or refreshing the page. Scanned pages need OCR, which isn’t included.
